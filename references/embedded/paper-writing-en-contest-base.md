# English contest paper-writing baseline

Read `paper-writing-mcm-icm-current.md` when the contest is MCM/ICM. This file is generic writing guidance and does not define current page, summary-sheet, AI, or submission requirements.

Use this module to turn verified modeling artifacts into a contest paper.

## Structure

Default structure:

1. Contest-required summary sheet or abstract
2. Problem restatement
3. Assumptions
4. Notation
5. Model design by subproblem
6. Solution process and algorithms
7. Results
8. Sensitivity or robustness analysis
9. Strengths and limitations
10. References
11. Appendices, if needed

Adapt to contest templates when provided.

## Writing Rules

- Define symbols before use.
- Keep formulas connected to plain-language purpose.
- Introduce figures and tables before or near their appearance.
- Tie every conclusion to a result, figure, table, or source.
- Do not invent numbers while writing.
- Keep limitations honest but not self-defeating.

## Abstract

The abstract should contain:

- problem objective
- core model or method
- main results
- validation or sensitivity finding
- practical conclusion

Write the abstract after results are verified.

## Paper Quality Gate

Before final verification:

- Every subproblem has a paper section or explicit answer.
- Assumptions are used consistently.
- Notation and variable names match code/results.
- Tables and figures are referenced.
- Citations support data, methods, or comparisons.
- The current contest branch has verified page counting, anonymity, AI disclosure, and submission requirements.
